What this is

There's magic in your neighborhood.

This isn't Angie's List. It isn't a directory or a paid listing service. It's a growing community of real local businesses — each one built with care, each one committed to doing good work and being a good neighbor.

When you join the Faery Tech network you're not just getting a website. You're getting a spot in something bigger — a shared story about what happens when small businesses support each other instead of just competing.

The community is free to join when you commission a site through Faery Tech. Ethical advertising across the network funds animal shelters, eco initiatives, and free web services for people who need them most.
